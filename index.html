How these numbers work

Each statement is scored from 0 to 3. The 21 statements are split into three groups of 7, one each for depression, anxiety and stress. Each group is added up and then doubled so the result matches the longer, 42-statement version of the scale. That is why every score runs from 0 to 42.

The labels below are bands used by clinicians to describe how far a score sits from the typical range. They describe how you felt in the last seven days. They do not describe who you are, and they can change.

BandDepressionAnxietyStress
Normal0–90–70–14
Mild10–138–915–18
Moderate14–2010–1419–25
Severe21–2715–1926–33
Extremely severe28+20+34+

“Severe” here describes the score relative to the general population. It is a signal to look closer and get support, and it is not a verdict about you.
